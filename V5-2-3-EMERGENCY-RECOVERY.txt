TS Розклад V5.2.3 — Emergency Admin Recovery

Added:
- Public /admin-recovery.html emergency recovery flow.
- One-time 6-digit email code, 10-minute expiry, 5 attempts.
- Rate limiting for recovery requests and verification.
- No account-enumeration response.
- New admin password is scrypt-hashed with a fresh salt.
- Old admin sessions are revoked after successful recovery.
- Recovery events are added to the admin audit log without storing the code.
- Added Ukrainian recovery email template.
- Added recovery link to admin toolbar.
